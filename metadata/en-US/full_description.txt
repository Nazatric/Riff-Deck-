RiFF Deck is a fast, offline local music player for Android with a rock/metal/Y2K skeuomorphic design — brushed steel panels, chrome transport controls, LCD-style readouts, and a spinning vinyl Now Playing screen, instead of another flat Material list.

Enjoy your favorite songs with word-by-word synced lyrics, translated lyric support (TTML/LRC), and a Now Playing screen built around album art with a spinning vinyl centerpiece and riveted control panel.

Fine-tune your listening with a built-in equalizer, ReplayGain, Audio Offload, and gapless playback for seamless transitions. Organize your music your way with smart playlists, customizable sorting, and a hierarchical folder view.

Main features:

* Automatic lyric download and in-app lyric editor
* Word-by-word and translated lyrics (TTML, LRC)
* Media3-based playback engine with audio offload support
* Bit-perfect playback support and native ALAC decoding
* Native ListenBrainz scrobbling
* Adaptive mini player and gesture controls
* Custom covers and descriptions for playlists
* Tag editor with instant library updates
* Smart auto playlists (Recently Played, Most Played, History)
* Sleep timer, widgets, and Android Auto support
* Rock/metal/Y2K skeuomorphic theming, always dark
* Multi-language support
* Lightweight, open-source, and ad-free
